Public liability insurance

Covered. Prepared. Professional.

Pro Active Lifeguard Services holds public liability insurance.

Why this matters to you

  • Financial backing for covered claims: Insurance can help meet compensation costs where we are legally liable for injury to a member of the public or damage to third-party property arising from our insured activities.
  • Confidence when booking: An additional layer of financial protection alongside our water-safety procedures.
  • Responsible service: Part of our commitment to managing risk professionally for events and venues.

Cover is subject to the policy terms, exclusions, excess and limits. Insurance does not guarantee that every incident or claim will be covered.
